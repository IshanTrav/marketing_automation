Where our own testing disagreed with the docsMeta's documentation implies Standard Access should cover reading a tester account's own comments on its own post — "own or manage" is the stated boundary. We tested this directly today (our own account commenting on its own post) and it still came back empty, via both the GET endpoint and a verified, subscribed webhook. Treat comment-reading as fully gated behind Advanced Access, full stop — don't rely on the "own account" exception holding in practice.

D

Business Verification — do this first

Required since Feb 2023 for any app requesting Advanced Access. Sequencing this after App Review is the most common self-inflicted delay.

  1. App Dashboard → Settings → Basic → Start Verification — routes into Business Manager's Security Center
  2. Submit one of: Certificate/Articles of Incorporation, Business Registration/License, a government business tax document, or a business bank statement (issued within the provider's stated window — commonly 90 days)
  3. A utility bill can support an address claim only — it cannot stand in for the legal business name
  4. Upload high-resolution (300 DPI+), uncropped scans
Most common rejection reasonThe legal name or address on the document doesn't exactly match what's entered in Business Manager — "Ltd" vs "Limited," a trading name instead of the legal name, "St" vs "Street." Copy the legal name character-for-character from the registration document into Business Manager before submitting anything.
Typical timeline: 3–7 business days, up to ~10 during Meta's quarterly policy-update windows.
E

App Review submission — per permission

Each permission needs three things submitted together, and reviewers work from the video, not a description of the feature.

  1. A screencast (1080p+, English UI, visible cursor) showing the complete journey: login → account connection → the specific feature that uses that one permission. One permission per video — don't bundle several into one clip.
  2. Written justification — a specific explanation of why this exact permission is needed for this exact feature, plus how the data is handled.
  3. A verifiable test call — at least one successful Graph API call using the permission, made within 30 days before submission (Graph API Explorer is acceptable).
Common rejection reasonFix
Requested a broader permission than the feature needsRequest the narrowest permission that covers the actual feature
Screencast skips steps or bundles permissionsOne clean, complete recording per permission
Privacy policy missing or brokenA real, loading URL, checked right before submitting
Missing Data Deletion Instructions URLAdd the callback/URL in App Settings before submitting
App still in Development ModeSwitch to Live before submitting for review
A known, real pain point — not our mistake if we hit itMeta's test-user creation flow has been reported broken for an extended period across many developers. The common workaround is a dedicated real "dummy" tester account added as an app role, used specifically for the review recording — not a violation of the process, just the practical way around a tool that isn't fully working on Meta's side.
Timeline — reported figures diverge: Meta's own guidance says ~1 week; multiple 2026 developer guides report Meta itself now setting expectations at ~20 days; real-world reports for Instagram publishing/messaging specifically describe 2–6 weeks with more than one round common. Plan for the long end.
F

Rules layered on top of the rate limits

These aren't capacity limits — they're hard policy rules that apply regardless of how much quota is left.

  • One automated Private Reply per comment, ever — and only within 7 days of the comment being posted
  • Automated messaging only within the 24-hour standard messaging window after the person's last interaction — outside that window needs different message tags or a human handoff
  • An automated conversation must disclose that it's automated — at the start of the thread, after a long gap, or when handing off from bot to human

What this means practicallyNothing blocks starting the real build today — Stage 08 can go straight to production on Standard Access. Stage 09 needs Business Verification (~1 week) then App Review (~1–6 weeks, realistically the longer end) run in parallel with the rest of the build, not after it. A same-day demo of the comment→DM idea for stakeholders can still use Meta Business Suite's native automation or a Meta-approved third party (ManyChat, etc.) — neither of those unblocks our own app's Advanced Access, they're separate, already-approved systems standing in for a live demo.
