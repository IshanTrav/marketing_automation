# PRODUCTION-READY GOOGLE VEO PROMPT: PERFORMANCE SHORT-FORM AD

**Format & Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 Vertical Video (1080x1920)  
**Total Duration:** 16 Seconds  
**Platform Target:** Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts  
**Target Audience:** Indian travelers aged 22–35, app-first, price-conscious, prone to multi-tab comparison fatigue.  
**Objective:** Drive direct response comment automation (`Comment TRAVAFA`) by highlighting the friction of tab-switching comparison paralysis versus single-flow flight search on Travafa.

---

### VISUAL & PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

- **Protagonist (Reference 1):** South Indian woman in her late twenties, wheatish complexion, dark wavy hair tied back neatly, small gold stud earrings, wearing a crisp cream linen shirt. Natural, expressive facial micro-reactions (fatigue, disbelief, focused clarity).
- **Phone Device (Reference 2):** Modern slim iPhone in a matte slate protective case, screen held at natural viewing angles.
- **Product UI (Reference 3 - Travafa App):** Authentic Travafa interface. Clean white and vibrant accent palette. Flight search module displaying fields: One-way / Round-trip toggle, Origin ("BLR - Bengaluru"), Destination ("DEL - New Delhi"), Departure Date, Number of Travellers ("1 Adult"), and Cabin Class ("Economy").
- **Environment:** Modern Bangalore apartment study / living area during early evening. Warm practical table lamp in background, soft natural ambient window fill light. Realistic home desk setting with a notebook and coffee cup in background.
- **Lighting:** Realistic, naturalistic indoor lighting with balanced warm practicals. No exaggerated cinematic bloom, artificial glowing aura, or unrealistic lens flares.
- **Camera Style:** Handheld dynamic camera with authentic documentary micro-shake during problem beats; stable, crisp macro and over-the-shoulder framing for Travafa UI interactions. Fast optical transitions matching eye trace.
- **Audio Profile:** High-fidelity tactile sound effects (rapid screen swipe whooshes, UI selection clicks, app switching haptic ticks) layered over natural room tone and a subtle, high-energy modern lo-fi beat that drops into clean focus during the Travafa demonstration. Spoken dialogue is crisp, natural Indian English, audio-only (NO on-screen captions or subtitles).

### BEAT-BY-BEAT DIRECTED TIMELINE

#### [0.0s – 3.0s] — THE HOOK & INSTANT FRICTION
- **Visual Micro-Beats:**
  - `0.0s–1.2s`: Tight macro over-the-shoulder shot. Her thumb rapidly double-swipes through iOS app switcher showing 5+ different flight booking apps with mismatched search fields.
  - `1.2s–2.0s`: Rapid cut to close-up on her face; she squints at the screen, brow furrowed in disbelief.
  - `2.0s–3.0s`: Cut back to screen; she taps into the first app, but the price refreshes higher.
- **Spoken Dialogue (Audio Only):** "Wait... was this flight four thousand or six thousand on the other app?"
- **SFX:** Rapid flutter of app-switcher swipe sounds, alert chime, subtle low-frequency friction tone.
- **Camera Movement:** Handheld micro-push-in toward the screen, creating immediate claustrophobic multi-tab tension.

#### [3.0s – 6.5s] — ESCALATION & TAB FATIGUE
- **Visual Micro-Beats:**
  - `3.0s–4.5s`: Medium close-up. She rubs her forehead with her left hand while her right thumb keeps frantically switching tabs, losing her previous search dates.
  - `4.5s–5.5s`: Macro shot on phone screen: tabs reloading, dates resetting, inconsistent search filters creating visual chaos.
  - `5.5s–6.5s`: She looks directly up from the phone, exhausted, speaking to herself.
- **Spoken Dialogue (Audio Only):** "I have six tabs open, lost an hour, and haven't booked anything."
- **SFX:** Fast UI clicking, frustrated exhale, page-reload whoosh.
- **Camera Movement:** Quick whip pan from phone screen up to her expressive eye reaction.

#### [6.5s – 8.5s] — THE REALIZATION & PIVOT
- **Visual Micro-Beats:**
  - `6.5s–7.5s`: She pauses her thumb, swipes up to clear all background apps with a decisive snap.
  - `7.5s–8.5s`: She taps the Travafa app icon on her clean home screen.
- **Spoken Dialogue (Audio Only):** "Stop. I'm checking this in one place on Travafa."
- **SFX:** Crisp swipe-to-close audio, clean single app tap chime.
- **Camera Movement:** Smooth settling crane-in to over-the-shoulder macro framing on the iPhone display.

#### [8.5s – 13.0s] — TRAVAFA PRODUCT MECHANISM IN ACTION
- **Visual Micro-Beats:**
  - `8.5s–9.8s`: Travafa Flight Search screen loads instantly. Her index finger taps the "Round-trip" toggle; the UI responds with a smooth haptic feedback animation.
  - `9.8s–11.0s`: She taps Departure and Return dates on the clean calendar view; selected dates highlight cleanly without layout shifting.
  - `11.0s–12.0s`: She selects "1 Adult, Economy" and hits the primary search button.
  - `12.0s–13.0s`: Flight options populate in a unified, clean list. Her thumb smoothly scrolls down, comparing direct flight schedules and parameters effortlessly on one screen.
- **Spoken Dialogue (Audio Only):** "Round-trip, dates locked, all the flight options right here."
- **SFX:** Tactile calendar date selection clicks, crisp list-populate snap sound.
- **Camera Movement:** Locked, ultra-sharp macro shot on the phone display; UI elements remain 100% legible and physically grounded.

#### [13.0s – 16.0s] — TRANSFORMATION & CTA
- **Visual Micro-Beats:**
  - `13.0s–14.5s`: Medium shot. She smiles with genuine relief, holding the phone steady, nodding at the finalized travel selection.
  - `14.5s–16.0s`: Stable punch-in framing of the Travafa search interface on the iPhone screen alongside her smiling direct-to-camera eye contact.
- **Spoken Dialogue (Audio Only):** "Done. Stop chasing tabs. Comment TRAVAFA and check your next trip."
- **SFX:** Confident confirmation tone, upbeat musical resolve.
- **Camera Movement:** Smooth, confident pull-back to medium portrait framing.

### PRODUCT TRUTH & CONTINUITY CONSTRAINTS

1. **Feature Fidelity:** Only show verified Travafa capabilities (Flight search: one-way/round-trip, from/to city, depart date, travellers, cabin class). 
2. **Zero Price Fabrication:** DO NOT generate artificial price tags, fake discount percentages, fake rupee amounts, or unsupported "guaranteed lowest price" badges.
3. **No Subtitles / Captions:** ABSOLUTELY NO on-screen transcription, subtitles, kinetic text overlays, speech bubbles, or auto-caption text boxes. All dialogue occurs as spoken natural voiceover / sync-sound audio only.
4. **Visual Consistency:** Ensure the protagonist strictly maintains the Reference 1 persona (South Indian woman, wheatish skin, tied wavy hair, cream linen shirt, gold studs) across every single frame. Phone hardware must match Reference 2 (slate-cased iPhone).
