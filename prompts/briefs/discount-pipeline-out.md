### 1. CORE CREATIVE CONCEPT & NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE
* **Topic:** Stop losing hours comparing flights across multiple tabs; compare available options cleanly in one place on Travafa.
* **Target Audience:** Indian travellers aged 22–35, app-first, price-conscious, prone to comparison paralysis and multi-tab exhaustion.
* **Core Problem:** The viewer spends 40+ minutes jumping between booking platforms and browser tabs chasing a better price, only to lose the original fare, forget which site had the better timing, and end up overwhelmed.
* **Product Solution:** Travafa’s unified Flight Search interface allows travellers to search round-trip or one-way routes, input dates and passenger/cabin details, and compare flights seamlessly in a single structured list.
* **Emotional Arc:** Frantic Confusion / Tab Overload → Annoyance & Exhaustion → Clean Discovery → Confident Decision → Direct Call to Action.
